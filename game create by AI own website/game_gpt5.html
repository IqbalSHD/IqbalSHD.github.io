How to Play

Survive as long as possible in the arena. Collect ⚚ Cores to level up and choose perks. Enemies scale over time. This experience is 100% single‑file and asset‑free (procedural audio/FX). Works offline once loaded.

Desktop Controls

  • Move: WASD / Arrow Keys
  • Aim: Mouse
  • Fire: Left Click / Space
  • Dash: Shift
  • Pause: P
  • Restart: R

Mobile Controls

  • Left joystick to move
  • Tap/hold FIRE to shoot
  • Double‑tap anywhere to dash

Perks

  • Rate Up, Damage Up, Multi‑Shot, Orbitals, Magnet, Auto‑Aim, Shield

Pro Tips

  • Keep momentum; kiting is king.
  • Use dash to break encirclement.
  • Perk into sustain (Magnet/Shield) for late game.

Accessibility: honors prefers-reduced-motion, high‑contrast palette, scalable UI, screen‑reader labels, and keyboard‑first focus order.
