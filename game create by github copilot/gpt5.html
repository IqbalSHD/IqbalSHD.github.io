A slick, one-file arcade arena with buttery controls, neon particles, adaptive waves, touch and gamepad support. No installs, no assets, pure web.

Controls

WASD/Arrows move · Mouse to aim · Click/Space shoot · Shift dash · P pause

Mobile: left joystick to move, right button to fire. Gamepad: left stick + A fire, B dash.
